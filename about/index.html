ABOUT THE LAB_

TRYTHIS is a long-term personal playground for weird ideas.

THE RULES
Simple. Fast. Personal. Every experiment gets its own personality. Nothing has to become a startup. Nothing has to solve humanity.
THE METHOD
An idea appears. It gets built. If it works, it stays. If it fails, it gets archived. If it is pointless, even better.
THE LONG GAME
Build a collection of dozens or hundreds of tiny experiments over the years.
